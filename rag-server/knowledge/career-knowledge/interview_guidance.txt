INTERVIEW GUIDANCE

Interview preparation should be based on the responsibilities, requirements, skills, and qualifications described in the job advertisement.

Common interview preparation activities include:

- Understanding the organisation and role.
- Reviewing the responsibilities in the job description.
- Reviewing the required skills.
- Preparing examples of relevant experience.
- Preparing explanations of relevant projects.
- Practising behavioural questions.
- Practising technical questions where appropriate.
- Preparing questions to ask the interviewer.

The STAR approach can be used for behavioural interview questions:

Situation:
Describe the relevant situation or context.

Task:
Explain the responsibility or objective.

Action:
Explain what actions were taken.

Result:
Explain the outcome.

Interview questions should be relevant to the selected job.

If the job advertisement requests a particular skill, qualification, technology, or type of experience, the assistant can suggest interview preparation around that requirement.

The assistant should not assume that a user has experience with a technology, qualification, or responsibility unless the user provides that information.

AUSTRALIAN CAREER GUIDANCE

Australian job advertisements commonly describe employment using terms such as:

- Full-time
- Part-time
- Casual
- Contract
- Temporary
- Graduate
- Internship
- Junior
- Entry-level

Australian job advertisements may also describe work arrangements such as:

- On-site
- Hybrid
- Remote

Users should check the individual job advertisement for the exact meaning and conditions associated with the position.

Australian job advertisements may specify qualifications using terms such as:

- Bachelor's degree
- Master's degree
- Diploma
- Certificate
- Relevant qualification
- Equivalent experience

A qualification described as required should not automatically be treated as optional.

A qualification described as preferred or desirable should be distinguished from a mandatory requirement.

Internships and graduate positions may place greater emphasis on education, projects, transferable skills, communication skills, willingness to learn, and relevant coursework than positions requiring extensive professional experience.

The assistant should rely on the selected job advertisement when explaining the actual requirements of a specific role.

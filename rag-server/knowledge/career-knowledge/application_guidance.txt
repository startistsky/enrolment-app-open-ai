JOB APPLICATION GUIDANCE

Before applying for a job, candidates can review the information provided in the job advertisement.

Important job information may include:

- Job title.
- Company or organisation.
- Location.
- Employment type.
- Work arrangement.
- Job description.
- Responsibilities.
- Required qualifications.
- Required skills.
- Preferred skills.
- Experience requirements.
- Salary information when available.
- Application instructions.
- Application deadline when provided.
- Application URL when available.

A candidate can use this information to understand what the employer is requesting and what information may be relevant to an application.

The assistant can help candidates:

- Understand the job advertisement.
- Summarise responsibilities.
- Identify required skills.
- Identify required qualifications.
- Identify experience requirements.
- Identify important terminology.
- Prepare application information.
- Prepare questions about the role.
- Prepare for an interview.
- Understand what information may be useful in an application.

The assistant should clearly distinguish information directly stated in the job advertisement from general career guidance.

Required qualifications should not automatically be treated as optional.

Preferred or desirable qualifications should be distinguished from mandatory requirements.

If an application URL is available, the candidate should follow the employer's stated application process.

The assistant should not claim that an application has been submitted unless the application system actually records that action.

The assistant should not invent application requirements, deadlines, documents, company procedures, or application stages.

If information is missing from the job advertisement, the assistant should state that the information is not available rather than inventing an answer.

Candidates should ensure that information provided in an application accurately represents their education, skills, experience, and qualifications.

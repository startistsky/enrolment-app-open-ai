ATS GUIDANCE

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software systems that employers may use to collect, organise, search, and manage job applications.

When preparing application documents for a job, users can improve clarity by using terminology that accurately reflects the job advertisement and their actual experience.

Useful practices include:

- Use clear section headings.
- Use readable and consistent formatting.
- Clearly describe relevant skills.
- Use terminology from the job advertisement when it accurately describes the applicant's experience.
- Include relevant qualifications and certifications.
- Describe relevant experience using specific and truthful language.
- Avoid adding keywords that do not accurately represent the applicant's background.

Keyword matching should not be treated as a guarantee that an application will pass an ATS.

A keyword appearing in a job advertisement does not mean an applicant should claim that skill. The applicant should only include skills and experience they genuinely possess.

When analysing a job advertisement, ATS-related guidance can identify important terms and concepts in the advertisement.

When no resume is available, the assistant can identify important job keywords but cannot determine whether the user's resume contains those keywords.

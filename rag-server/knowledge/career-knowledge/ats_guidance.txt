ATS GUIDANCE

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software systems that organisations may use to collect, organise, and manage job applications.

ATS systems may process information contained in application documents such as resumes and application forms.

General ATS preparation guidance includes:

- Use clear and standard section headings in a resume.
- Make the resume easy to read.
- Clearly state education, skills, experience, projects, and qualifications.
- Use terminology that accurately reflects the job advertisement.
- Include relevant technical skills when the candidate genuinely has those skills.
- Include relevant qualifications when the candidate genuinely holds those qualifications.
- Describe relevant projects and experience clearly.
- Avoid adding skills or qualifications that the candidate does not have.
- Ensure that dates, job titles, education details, and other information are accurate.
- Proofread the application before submission.

When tailoring a resume for a particular job, candidates can compare the resume with the job advertisement.

Useful areas to compare include:

- Job title.
- Required qualifications.
- Required technical skills.
- Preferred skills.
- Experience requirements.
- Responsibilities.
- Technologies and tools.
- Relevant projects.
- Relevant coursework.

Keywords from a job advertisement should only be used when they accurately describe the candidate's actual skills, qualifications, education, or experience.

Candidates should not add keywords solely to make an application appear more relevant if the candidate does not actually possess the corresponding skill or qualification.

ATS optimisation should not involve misleading information.

The assistant should not claim that a particular employer uses a specific ATS unless reliable information establishes that fact.

The assistant should not guarantee that a resume will pass an ATS.

ATS guidance should supplement, not replace, accurate and readable application content.

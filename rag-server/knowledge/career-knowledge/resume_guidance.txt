RESUME GUIDANCE

A resume should present information that is relevant to the position being applied for.

A resume commonly contains:

- Contact information.
- Professional summary or profile.
- Education.
- Employment history.
- Relevant technical skills.
- Relevant transferable skills.
- Projects.
- Certifications.
- Achievements or relevant activities.

For a particular job application, resume content should be aligned with the job advertisement.

Important job-related terms and skills appearing in the job advertisement can help identify areas that should be clearly represented in a resume when they genuinely match the applicant's background.

A resume should not claim skills, qualifications, experience, certifications, or achievements that the applicant does not have.

When recommending resume changes, distinguish between:

- Information that should be emphasised because it is relevant to the job.
- Information that could be added if the applicant genuinely has that experience.
- Information that should not be invented.

If the career assistant does not have access to the user's resume or profile, it should not claim that the user's resume contains or lacks a particular skill.

Instead, it should provide general guidance based on the selected job's requirements.
